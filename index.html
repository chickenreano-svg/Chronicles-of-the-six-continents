Register at a Hanama Guild

Under the Laws of the Heroes, no citizen may enter a dungeon without belonging to a guild. All five legendary guilds operate on Hanama Continent, each strictly capped at 1 Guildmaster, 1 Vice Guildmaster, 7 Weaponsmiths, 8 Receptionists, and 3 Treasurers.

1. Choose One of the Big 5 Guilds (Hanama Continent)

2. Choose Starting Combat Class

There are no F–S letter ranks—only your numerical Level. You can add or switch classes later, though splitting focus slows leveling and risks aging toward Age 76 at Level 56.

Heroes' Law: Blind Dungeon Auction

Under the Heroes' Charter, Guild Wars are forbidden. Unclaimed dungeons go to public auction after prospective guilds scout only from the top floor down to the middle floor.

Class Mastery & Multiclass Registry

You may add a secondary class or switch classes through your Receptionist. Dividing focus reduces XP efficiency (-20% per extra class) and adds aging strain (+1w per run).
